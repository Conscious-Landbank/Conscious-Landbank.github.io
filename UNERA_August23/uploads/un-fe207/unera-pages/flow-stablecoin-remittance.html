Flow 2 — Stablecoin Remittance (Send)

7-step send flow: prerequisites check, method selection, recipient entry, amount, review, processing, and outcome. Includes happy path, edge cases, all 9 error outcomes, and 2 missing features.

Sub-features

  • 1Send crypto to wallet address
  • 2Payee wallet management — Missing
  • 3Crypto-to-Crypto matching for cashing — Missing
  • 4Transfer confirmation / receipt
