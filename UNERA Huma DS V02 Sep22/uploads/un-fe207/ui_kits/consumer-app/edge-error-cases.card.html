What this section covers

Every non-happy path the consumer app must handle: loading, empty, partial-failure, unsupported-network, field-validation, identity-blocked, and terminal-failure states. Sources: wallet-edge.html, send-enhanced.html, dashboard-kyc-blocked.html, dashboard-kyc-retry.html. Rule: failure and success are different components — never recolor one into the other. Errors use --error / --surface-error-soft; cautions use --warning / --surface-warning-soft; money direction stays on --fin-up / --fin-down.

Identity (KYC) alert banners — gradient banner, Deep Blue CTA with Yellow label

Account-level blockers shown above the dashboard. Blocked (under review) offers only a support contact; retry (more info needed) offers a resume CTA. Both use role="alert" + aria-live.

Identity verification unavailable
You're not able to complete identity verification on this account while we review it. Please contact support for next steps.
Contact support
Verification needs another try
Additional information is needed to complete your identity check. Please resume the verification flow to resubmit.
Loading — skeleton placeholders (shimmer, reduced-motion safe)

Portfolio stats + token rows while balances resolve. Animation gated by prefers-reduced-motion. Marked aria-hidden; a separate live region announces "Loading balances".

Empty states — dashed panel on --surface-sky, low-opacity icon, primary recovery action

No tokens yet

Your wallet is connected, but we didn't find any UNERA-supported tokens on this network.

No transactions found

Try adjusting your filters or search terms, or start by adding funds to your wallet.

Partial failure — inline sync banner (soft error, non-blocking)

Balances couldn't refresh but the page is still usable. Quiet --surface-error-soft banner with a retry — distinct from the full error panel below.

Couldn't load tokens
Some balances may be out of date. Check your connection and try again.
Hard failure — full error & timeout panels (asymmetric to success; no celebration hero)

Couldn't load tokens

Check your internet connection and try again. If the problem continues, your network may be temporarily unavailable.

This is taking longer than usual

Loading balances took too long. Check your connection and retry — your funds are safe.

Unsupported / wrong network — caution banner, switch action

Wallet connected to a chain Huma doesn't support. --surface-warning-soft (caution, not failure) with a switch CTA. Mirrors the connect-modal wrong-network scenario.

Wrong network for tokens
Switch to Base or Sepolia to load supported tokens. Huma doesn't support the currently selected network.
Field validation — inline errors (Send), aria-invalid + descriptive message

Validation lives under the field, error-colored, announced via aria-describedby. The most common Send edge cases: insufficient balance, malformed address, and sending to your own address.

Insufficient balance — you have 2,500.00 hUSD available.
Enter a valid wallet address (0x followed by 40 hex characters).
You can't send to your own wallet address.
On-chain transfers are irreversible
Double-check the recipient. Once confirmed, a transfer can't be undone or refunded by Huma.
