How Our Reserves Work

UNERA maintains full transparency by holding reserves that equal or exceed the total value of all hCAD stablecoins in circulation. Our reserves are independently audited monthly to ensure complete backing.

Secure Storage
Reserves held in regulated financial institutions and verified stablecoins

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Proof of Reserve means that UNERA holds assets equal to or greater than the total value of all hCAD stablecoins in circulation. This ensures that every token is fully backed by real assets.

  • Our reserves are independently audited monthly by certified third-party auditors. Audit reports are published publicly and available for download.

  • We maintain a buffer above 100% to ensure stability. If the ratio approaches 100%, automatic safeguards prevent new token issuance until reserves are restored. However, with our current 105.2% ratio, we have a healthy safety margin.

  • Reserves are held in regulated Canadian financial institutions, verified USDC stablecoins, government treasury bonds, and highly liquid cash equivalents. All holdings are segregated and audited regularly.

  • Yes, hCAD tokens can be redeemed for Canadian dollars at any time through our withdrawal process. The redemption is processed within 1-3 business days.

  • Reserve data is updated in real-time on this page. You can also download monthly audit reports and verify on-chain data through blockchain explorers.
