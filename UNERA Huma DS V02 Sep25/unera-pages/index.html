The Endless Impact Loop

Your donation is kept. The interest it earns is what gets spent.

1

You Contribute

Convert your funds into hCAD, our Canadian dollar-backed stablecoin, or pay by card. Either way the donation is recorded on-chain, and you can look it up.

2

Capital Secured

The donation itself sits in audited, regulated reserves. The principal is never spent. Only the interest it earns pays for community programs.

3

Yield Generated

The reserves are held in low-risk instruments. What they return each year is the budget for programs, and your original gift stays where it is.

4

Centers Funded

That yield goes to verified Humanity Centers. Because it arrives every month, a center can plan a year ahead instead of quarter to quarter.

5

Lives Changed

Schooling, clinics and childcare keep running because the funding does not stop at the end of a campaign.

6

Impact Attracts More

Published results bring in more donors and partners. The reserve grows, and so does the yield it pays out.
