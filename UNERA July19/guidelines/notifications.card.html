Notification levels

Completed · --fin-up
Action finished successfully — buy/trade/swap done.
In progress · --brand-deep-blue
A downstream user-visible step has started.
Info · --fin-neutral
Neutral update — service announcement, maintenance.
Warning · --warning
Attention needed or delayed — not a final failure.
Error · --error
Failure or blocked action — no sensitive reason codes.

Color maps to level, never alone — always paired with icon + text label.
